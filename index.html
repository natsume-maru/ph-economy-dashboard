💡 What is GDP in Simple Terms?

The Household Analogy

The Nation's Total Report Card

Imagine your country as a single giant household. Just as you add up every salary, business earnings, and side hustle in your family to get your total monthly household income, **GDP (Gross Domestic Product)** is the total market value of all goods and services produced inside the country in a year.

The Formula

How GDP is Calculated

Economists measure national economic output using four key pillars:

GDP = C + I + G + (X - M)
  • C (Consumption): Household spending (groceries, utilities, dining)
  • I (Investment): Business capital, equipment, construction
  • G (Government Spending): Public roads, health, education, defense
  • (X - M) Net Exports: Exports minus Imports

👥 What's in it for You as a Filipino?

Demographics & Workforce

114 Million Citizens & Growth Impact

The Philippines has a young, fast-growing population with over 52.5 million active workers (64.8% participation rate).

  • Jobs & Quality of Work: Higher GDP growth means businesses expand, creating formal wage jobs for the millions entering the workforce.
  • Purchasing Power: Growth must outpace inflation so family paychecks keep up with food, fuel, and housing costs.
  • Public Infrastructure: A larger total GDP expands national tax collections to fund hospitals, transportation, and public schools per citizen.
